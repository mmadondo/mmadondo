# Malvern Madondo
 
Postdoctoral Associate at [Duke University](https://surgery.duke.edu/) · Department of Surgery
 
I develop **reinforcement learning** and **control** methods for decision-making in healthcare, and the oversight that keeps adaptive, multi-agent AI systems within safe limits.
 
Previously, I was a Research Scientist at the University of Chicago, building patient-specific deep RL for adaptive proton therapy. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from Emory University, where I was advised by [Lars Ruthotto](https://www.math.emory.edu/~lruthot/) and supported by a [Google PhD Fellowship](https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/recipients/?filtertab=2021).
 
---
 
### Research Interests
 
- **Oversight of clinical AI**: supervisory control for adaptive and multi-agent systems
- **Offline decision-making**: safety-constrained learning from clinical data
- **RL for treatment planning**: patient-specific deep RL for radiation therapy
- **Closed-loop biomedical control**: neuromodulation and glycemic control
- **Optimal control + ML**: neural ODEs, Pontryagin's Maximum Principle, HJB equations
